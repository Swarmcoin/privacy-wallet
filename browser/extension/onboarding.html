Set up your wallet

Your keys stay on this computer, in a program outside the browser. The extension can see your balance and ask the program to pay someone; it never holds your recovery phrase.

Step 1 of 3

Before you create it

Your wallet is a recovery phrase of 24 words. Anyone who reads those words can spend your coins, and nobody — not the project, not this computer — can recover them for you if you lose them. Write them on paper.
Step 2 of 3

Write these 24 words down

They are shown once. Closing this page before you have written them down means losing the wallet.

Restore a wallet

Type the 24 words of your recovery phrase, separated by spaces.

The birthday is the block the wallet starts scanning from. Leave it empty to scan the whole chain, which is slower but never misses anything.
