What this extension can and cannot do

It has no permission to read any web page. It makes one kind of network request of its own: while the popup is open on SWARM Mainnet, it asks the SWARM price service (wallet.swarm.green) for the SWM price, once a minute. That request carries no address, no balance and no identifier, and you can switch it off below. Everything else it shows comes from the wallet host on this computer, and the host is the only thing that talks to the SWARM network. Your recovery phrase never enters the browser.

The wallet file on disk is not encrypted with a passphrase. It is protected by your Windows account and by Windows Hello, which is asked for before unlocking and again before every payment.

SWM price

The price comes from the SWARM price service (wallet.swarm.green), which reads the SWM/ETH pool on Base from GeckoTerminal and DexScreener. Your addresses and balances are never sent. Switch this off and the wallet makes no price requests.

Shown in the popup on SWARM Mainnet only. It is an indicative price: the pool is small, and small trades move it. It is not a quote.

SWARM Rewards

Receive address …

SWARM Rewards is a separate extension. When it is allowed, it may ask this wallet for your receive address and for nothing else: not your recovery phrase, not a key, not your balance, not your history. You are asked once, in the popup, and the answer is kept here.

Advanced

If you have been asked to test on the SWARM test network, type testnet below. Test coins have no value. Lock the wallet first.

This build can be pointed at either SWARM network. It will not move your existing wallet.
