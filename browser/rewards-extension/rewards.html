A day counts once, the first time the browser is used that day while you are taking part. The day is the one on your own calendar.

Linked wallet
no wallet linked

Linking asks the SWARM Wallet extension for your receive address. The wallet asks you first, once, and never hands over your recovery phrase, a key, your balance or your history. You can stop sharing at any time in the wallet's Settings.

What is kept on this computer

  • Whether you are taking part.
  • How many days you have been active, as a number.
  • The last day that number was raised, so today is not counted twice.
  • Your receive address, once you link it.

What is not kept, anywhere

  • The sites you visit, their addresses, or anything derived from them.
  • The times of day you browse, or how long for.
  • Any identifier that could follow you between computers.

This extension has no permission to read a web page and makes no network request of any kind. Its content security policy sets connect-src 'none', so the browser would refuse one.

Forget everything

Sets the day count back to zero, unlinks your wallet and turns taking part off. Nothing is kept anywhere else, so there is nothing else to delete.

There are no invitations, no tiers and no bonuses for bringing other people. There never will be.
